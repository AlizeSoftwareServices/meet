💡 1. The Core Concept (Why Meet Exists)

❌ The Old Friction-Filled Way

Arranging a meeting manually requires endless back-and-forth messaging across emails, WhatsApp, or phone calls:

  • "Are you free tomorrow at 3 PM?"
  • "No, I have another meeting. How about Thursday at 11 AM?"
  • "Thursday I'm traveling. What about Friday?"
  • Result: Wasted time, forgotten meetings, timezone mix-ups, and double-bookings.

✅ The Meet Autonomous Way

Meet replaces all communication friction with a single intelligent, self-service link:

  • You connect your calendar once (Google Calendar or Outlook).
  • You share your link: meet.alizesoftwareservices.com/book/username
  • The guest picks an available slot that automatically fits around your existing schedule.
  • Instant Confirmation: Video link generated, calendars synced, emails delivered.

⚡ 2. Complete End-to-End Workflow

1

Host Setup & Real-time Integration

The Host logs into Meet, connects Google Calendar via OAuth2 in the Integrations tab, and defines their working hours (e.g. Mon–Fri, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM).

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: நீங்கள் உங்கள் Google Calendar-ஐ இணைத்து வேலை நேரத்தை பிக்ஸ் செய்கிறீர்கள்.
2

One-Click Sharing (Profile or Specific Event)

The Host copies their personal public booking link from the top-right corner of the Dashboard and shares it on WhatsApp, Email, LinkedIn, or SMS.

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: உங்கள் Top-Right பட்டன் மூலம் Booking Link-ஐ வாட்ஸ்அப் அல்லது சமூக வலைத்தளங்களில் பகிர்கிறீர்கள்.
3

Dynamic Availability Calculation & Timezone Conversion

When the Guest opens the link on their mobile or computer, Meet queries the Host's Google Calendar in real time (`freebusy` API). Any busy time is automatically hidden. The times are converted automatically to the Guest's local timezone (e.g., US EST, London GMT, or IST).

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: கெஸ்ட் அந்த லிங்க்கை திறந்ததும், நீங்கள் ஃப்ரீயாக உள்ள நேரம் மட்டும் அவர்களது சொந்த நாட்டு நேரப்படி (Local Timezone) தானாகக் காட்டும்.
4

Zero-Friction Instant Booking

The Guest selects a date, chooses a time slot, enters their Name, Email, and any custom questions, and clicks "Schedule Event". The booking is instantly confirmed with optimistic concurrency control.

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: கெஸ்ட் நேரத்தைத் தொட்டு பெயர், மெயில் கொடுத்ததும் மீட்டிங் உடனடியாக உறுதியாகிவிடும் (Instant Confirmed).
5

Automated Video Injection & Calendar Sync

The backend automatically generates a Google Meet video conference link, writes the event to the Host's Google Calendar, sends an HTML confirmation with an `.ics` invite to the Guest, and delivers Push and Slack notifications to the Host.

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: கூகுள் மீட் வீடியோ லிங்க் உருவாகி, உங்கள் காலண்டரிலும் கெஸ்ட் மெயிலிலும் தானாகப் பதிவாகிவிடும்.
6

One-Click Reschedule / Cancellation Sync

If plans change, either party can reschedule or cancel using the secure token buttons. When rescheduled, the Google Calendar event shifts to the new time automatically without any manual intervention.

📌 தமிழ் விளக்கம்: மீட்டிங்கை மாற்றினாலோ அல்லது ரத்து செய்தாலோ உங்கள் Google Calendar-லிருந்து அது தானாகவே அப்டேட் ஆகிவிடும்.

📖 4. Step-by-Step Guide for the Host

Step 1: Connect Your Google Calendar

Go to Integrations in the sidebar. Click "Connect Google Calendar" and grant calendar permissions. Meet will now monitor your real-time busy slots to prevent overlap.

Step 2: Define Your Weekly Working Hours

Go to Availability. Check the days you work (e.g. Monday to Friday) and set your open hours (e.g., 09:00 to 17:00). You can also set a 15-minute buffer between meetings.

Step 3: Create Your Event Types

Go to Event Types → Click "+ Create Event Type". Choose a name like "30-Min Consultation", select 30 mins duration, pick "Google Meet" as location, and save.

Step 4: Share Your Link Anywhere

Click "Share Profile Link" in the top-right corner of your Dashboard. You can copy the link or directly click WhatsApp/LinkedIn to send it to clients.

🌟 5. What the Guest Sees and Experiences

The guest experience is designed to be frictionless, fast, and does NOT require downloading any app or creating an account:

1. Interactive Calendar View

The guest clicks your link and sees a clean calendar. Only dates where you have open slots are clickable.

2. Local Timezone Display

The app detects the guest's location and displays time slots in their local timezone so no manual conversion is needed.

3. Instant Email & .ics Invite

Once booked, the guest gets an email confirmation containing the Google Meet URL and a calendar attachment to auto-add to their phone calendar.

4. Self-Service Reschedule

If the guest has a conflict later, they click "Reschedule" in their email to choose a new slot without calling or messaging you.
